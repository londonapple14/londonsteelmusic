About

The man behind the music

London Steel is a singer, songwriter, and producer based in New York City. His music moves between R&B, indie folk, and rap — but the through-line never changes: honest songs about faith, love, and the everyday moments that shape a life.

Off the mic, he's a husband first. He and his wife are expecting their first child — a baby girl — and his feed has become a window into that journey: marriage, trusting God through hard seasons, and building a family in the city.

Every London Steel song is written and produced by London himself, from the first demo to the final mix. No shortcuts, no ghostwriters — just the work.
